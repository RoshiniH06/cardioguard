Healthy Living Tips for Your Heart

Small, consistent daily habits can significantly support your cardiovascular system and overall well-being.

🥗 1. Eat a Heart-Healthy Diet

Focus on a diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins. Limit saturated fats, trans fats, and excessive sodium (salt). The Mediterranean diet, emphasizing healthy fats like olive oil and nuts, is highly recommended.

🚶 2. Move Your Body Regularly

Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity (like brisk walking or cycling) per week, plus muscle-strengthening exercises twice a week to maintain optimal blood pressure.

⚖️ 3. Maintain a Healthy Weight

Keeping a healthy BMI and waist circumference significantly lowers the workload on your heart. Even a modest 5-10% weight optimization yields major vascular and metabolic benefits.

😴 4. Prioritize Quality Sleep

Most adults require 7 to 9 hours of restorative sleep each night. Untreated sleep disturbances like sleep apnea increase hypertension and cardiovascular stress.

🚭 5. Avoid Tobacco and Limit Alcohol

Smoking damages the endothelium of arterial walls and triggers plaque deposition. Quitting smoking is one of the single most impactful actions for cardiac longevity.

🧘 6. Manage Chronic Stress

High cortisol and chronic sympathetic activation elevate heart rate and blood pressure. Integrate mindfulness, meditation, breathing drills, or nature walks.

🩺 7. Monitor Your Key Biomarkers

Regularly track blood pressure, lipid panels (LDL, HDL, triglycerides), and fasting blood glucose to catch early metabolic warning signs before complications arise.

💧 8. Maintain Optimal Hydration

Proper hydration supports blood volume and eases circulatory efficiency. Replace high-sugar beverages with water and herbal infusions.

Emergency Note: These recommendations are for lifestyle wellness. If you experience crushing chest pressure, shortness of breath, or numbness, call emergency medical services immediately.

About CardioGuard AI

CardioGuard bridges machine learning diagnostics and preventative patient engagement, delivering instant risk stratification based on verified epidemiological survey data.

📊 The BRFSS Clinical Dataset

CardioGuard is trained on the CDC's Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS) dataset, one of the world's premier continuous health surveys.

  • Scale: 308,000+ validated survey participants.
  • Features: 19 lifestyle and clinical variables (BMI, age group, diabetes history, physical activity, dietary patterns).
  • Target: Clinically identified cardiovascular disease occurrences.

🤖 Machine Learning Architecture

The backend uses an optimized Histogram-based Gradient Boosting Classifier with tuned thresholding to optimize patient safety.

  • Accuracy: ~91.9% test performance.
  • ROC-AUC: 0.836 discriminative ability.
  • Recall Prioritization: Calibrated decision threshold (0.18) ensures high sensitivity, preventing high-risk patients from receiving false negatives.

💻 Technical Stack

Built as a modern, high-performance web platform:

  • Frontend: Responsive UI with CSS glassmorphism, vector medical iconography, and vanilla JS.
  • Backend API: FastAPI and Uvicorn.
  • ML Toolkit: Scikit-Learn, Pandas, NumPy, and Joblib.
  • Client AI: Tesseract.js client OCR for automated medical form scanning & jsPDF for instant clinical reports.
⚠️ Important Disclaimer: CardioGuard is an educational and analytical research demonstration. It does not provide medical diagnoses or treatment advice. Consult licensed medical personnel for health management.
