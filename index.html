Healthy Living Tips

Small, consistent habits can support your heart and overall well-being. Start with changes that are realistic for you.

🥗 1. Eat a balanced diet

Choose a variety of vegetables, fruits, whole grains, beans, nuts and other minimally processed foods. Limit excess salt, sugary drinks and highly processed foods.

🚶 2. Move your body regularly

Aim for around 150 minutes of moderate activity, such as brisk walking, each week if it is safe for you. Start gradually and include movement breaks during long periods of sitting.

💧 3. Stay hydrated

Drink water regularly throughout the day. Your needs can vary with weather, activity and health conditions; follow your clinician's advice if you have fluid restrictions.

😴 4. Get enough sleep

Keep a consistent sleep and wake time. Most adults need about 7–9 hours of sleep each night.

🚭 5. Avoid tobacco and limit alcohol

Do not smoke or use tobacco. Avoiding alcohol is the healthiest choice for many people; if you drink, discuss personal risks with a healthcare professional.

🩺 6. Know your health numbers

Have your blood pressure, cholesterol and blood glucose checked as recommended. Keep a record and discuss unusual results with a qualified healthcare professional.

🧘 7. Manage stress

Try relaxing activities such as slow breathing, stretching, time outdoors or talking with someone you trust. Seek professional support if stress feels difficult to manage.

Remember: These are general wellness tips, not a personal treatment plan. If you have a medical condition or symptoms, ask a qualified healthcare professional for advice. Seek urgent medical help for chest pain, severe shortness of breath or fainting.

About CardioGuard

CardioGuard is an academic prototype of an early cardiovascular disease risk prediction portal developed for the Project-Based End-Semester Assessment (BCA 1st Semester, November 2026).

Project Team (Group C)

  • Roshini Singha: Team Leader; Machine Learning Pipeline & Comparative Evaluation (SVC & KNN)
  • Gyanendra Deka: Backend Development; FastAPI Architecture & Inference Routes
  • Madhurjya Dewraja: Data Preparation, Cleaning, Quality Checks & EDA
  • Manash Sarma: Frontend Development, UI/UX, Dynamic Dashboard & Biomarkers
  • Faculty Mentor: Mr. Soumyajit Jalua (Scholars Institute of Technology and Management)

What this system provides

Patient Intake: Interactive health screening form accepting age, gender, height, weight, systolic & diastolic BP, cholesterol, glucose, smoking, alcohol, and activity.

Multi-Model Prediction: Real-time inference calling trained pipelines: Support Vector Classifier (SVC), K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), and HistGradientBoosting ensemble.

Health Calculators: Real-time Blood Pressure classification (AHA criteria) and BMI calculator (CDC criteria).

Clinical Reports: Printable and downloadable PDF health summaries with personalized preventive guidance.

Dataset & Methodology

Dataset: 70,000 patient records from the Cardiovascular Disease dataset. Cleaned of physiological outliers (68,586 valid records retained). Stratified 80/20 train/test split.

Pipeline Design: scikit-learn ColumnTransformers with StandardScaler fitted strictly on training folds to prevent data leakage.

Non-Diagnostic Medical Notice

CardioGuard is strictly an educational screening demonstration and academic project. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose disease, and cannot replace a qualified clinician's assessment. Consult a doctor for personal health concerns.
